# ZettaLabs

Engineering for research and science: connectors, data pipelines, reproducible builds, platforms and tailored tooling for research teams.

- **Interline** (https://interline.zettalabs.co.nz): a worldwide registry of science funding programmes, open data under CC BY 4.0, readable by people and AI agents.
- **Builds**: public fixes, reproducible environments, CI and connectors for open-source research code.

Based in Auckland, New Zealand. Contact: hello@zettalabs.co.nz
